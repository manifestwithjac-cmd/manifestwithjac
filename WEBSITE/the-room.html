Where the work actually is

Knowing was never the fucking problem.

You already know what your next level self would do. Every coach on the internet has asked you that question, and you can answer it in your sleep. She raises the price. She says no to that client. She posts the thing. She leaves the offer alone long enough for it to work.

Great. You know.

The real question is what happens inside you when current you goes to make that decision. Today. With today’s bank balance. Before anybody has clapped.

Because every one of those decisions is easy once the evidence is in. Anybody can charge more when the inquiries are stacked up. Anybody can turn down a bad client when a better one is already booked. Anybody can hold the price while the sales are rolling.

Can you make the move first and let the move work?

This is where regulating yourself gets useful, and I want to be specific about it because the internet has made it stupid. Calming down is not a payment method. Panicked women make a lot of money every single day. Getting out of panic is not what causes the money.

Getting out of panic is what lets you hear yourself clearly enough to make a sound decision instead of a scared one.

Calm yourself down. Then discern. Then decide. Then move. Not calm yourself down, then wait, then hope.

You decide the destination. You don’t dictate the route.

How I actually think

Let me walk you through one.

The client

A baker. Call her Taylor. She is doing somewhere around $10K to $12K a month. Real business, real orders, real customers. And she keeps saying the same sentence to me.

“All these clients are so fucking cheap.”

The diagnosis

Bitch, no, they’re not. They are not cheap. You are marketing yourself to cheap people. You are taking on the cheap people. You are allowing that to happen.

Every post is a six inch cake. Every post is the cheapest thing she makes. No pricing anywhere, so everybody who was never going to spend $900 is in her inbox, and everybody who would have is scrolling past with no reason to stop.

The move

Start posting to the clients that you want to book you. Show the cakes you want more people to order. Put the pricing on the cakes. Let the price sort people so you stop doing it by hand, for free, in your inbox, at 11pm.

That part takes me about ten minutes to explain. Most people could have told her that.

That is the coaching. Not the cake pricing.
