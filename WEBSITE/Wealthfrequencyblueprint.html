1
Step One — The Foundation

Get clear on the exact dollar amount

Most women skip this step or do it half-heartedly. Your subconscious cannot deliver what you can't articulate. Clarity is a frequency. The more specific the amount, the timeline, and the lifestyle it funds — the faster the universe can rearrange to match it.

Don't just write "I want more money." Write $40K this month. Write $10K extra in the next 30 days. Write the exact balance you want sitting in your account on December 31st. Then write what your life looks like with it. Specifics. Receipts. Sensations.

If you can't feel the money in your body, you can't receive it.
2
Step Two — The Awareness

Notice what comes up around money

The second you decide on the amount, your subconscious will start showing you every reason why you can't have it. The panic about debt. The shame around past money decisions. The "people like me don't make that." The fear of being judged for wanting more. This isn't proof you can't have it. It's the exact opposite. It's your subconscious revealing the money blocks that need to clear before the amount can come to you.

Don't suppress what comes up. Notice it. That awareness IS the work.

What comes up is what's coming out.

It usually means the block lives deeper than your conscious mind can access — generational money trauma, debt shame, or programming installed before you had words for it. Surface-level methods can't move it. Step Three — The Identity

Assume the money is already yours

This is the law of assumption — and it's the piece most people get completely wrong with money. Here's where the nuance lives, because this matters more than anything else in this blueprint:

Embodying her is not "she would buy this $300 thing so let me put it on the credit card." That's not assumption. That's performance. That's racking up debt to prove a frequency you haven't actually moved into yet. And it backfires every single time.

The wealthy version of you doesn't buy things to prove she's wealthy. She already feels wealthy. She doesn't need the receipt. She doesn't need the bag, the dress, the trip on a credit card to confirm her identity. Her wealth is internal first — and the external follows.

So the question is never "what would she buy?" The question is "how does she feel?" And the answer is always the same: calm. Certain. Free. Relaxed. Unbothered by the number in the account because she knows the money is always flowing.

When you tap into that feeling — when your nervous system is in that frequency — then aligned action shows up naturally. Sometimes it's buying the thing because you actually want it. Sometimes it's not buying it because she doesn't need to. The feeling came first. The action came from the feeling. Not the other way around.

You don't get the money you want. You get the feeling you're holding.

Here's the truth nobody tells you: you will never manifest from a panicked nervous system. "Where's my $1,000?? I need it by Friday!!" — that frequency cannot pull money in. Your body is broadcasting "I'm not safe, I'm not safe, I'm not safe" and the universe is matching it. That's the law of vibration. Like attracts like.

The work is not "manifest the $1,000." The work is regulate first. Drop into your body. Breathe. Tap. Whatever it takes to come back to "I am safe. I am supported. I will be okay no matter what happens." Lean all the way into that. Let your body actually feel it. Let your shoulders drop. Let your jaw unclench.

Because here's what happens when you regulate: your brain comes back online. Suddenly you remember the family member you could call. The community resource you saw last week. The side income you forgot about. The unexpected refund. The opportunity that was already there but invisible to you when you were in fight-or-flight. The money doesn't show up because you panicked harder. It shows up because you regulated enough to receive it.

You don't manifest by white-knuckling the outcome. You manifest by knowing — in your body — that you're safe. That's the work. Always.
