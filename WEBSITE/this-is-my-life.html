01

What do you actually want?

Not what would be reasonable.

Not what somebody in your industry says is good.

Not the number you think you’re allowed to say without sounding delusional.

What do you actually want?

What do you want the business to make? Who do you want to work with? What do you want your days to look like? What do you want money to make possible? What does a random Tuesday look like?

02

What are you currently making normal?

What are you expecting without realizing you’re expecting it?

What happens when sales get quiet? What kind of client do you automatically expect? What price feels normal? What opportunity feels “too big”?

What have you repeated so many times that it stopped sounding like a belief and started sounding like a fact?

Because what you normalize changes the way you run the business. And eventually, the way you run the business changes what becomes normal.

03

What’s actually in the way?

Maybe it’s strategy. Maybe it’s subconscious. Maybe it’s an old experience you turned into a prediction.

Maybe you’re scared to be seen. Maybe the offer genuinely needs work. Maybe your marketing needs work.

Maybe you’re running away from discomfort instead of moving toward the solution.

We figure out what the actual problem is before we start throwing solutions at it.

04

Get yourself ready to choose differently.

Sometimes you already know exactly what you want to do.

You just need to stop reacting long enough to actually do it.

Not because you need to be perfectly healed before you’re allowed to make money.

Because I want the person making the decision to actually be YOU.

05

Make the fucking move.

Eventually, you have to move.

Make the decision. Take the step. Create new evidence. See what happens.

Respond to actual feedback. Adjust when adjustment is actually called for.

And keep going.

FAQ

Do I need to already have a business?

Yes.

This is for women who already have something real people pay them for.

You do not need to hit a specific revenue number before joining.

But you should have an actual business to bring into the room.

If you’re trying to figure out what business you should start, this isn’t the right room yet.

Is this business coaching or manifestation?

Both.

Because I don’t separate them.

The assumptions you hold about what’s available to you affect the way you price, market, sell, show up, interpret feedback and make decisions.

And your assumptions alone do not build the business.

Manifestation requires action.

We work with the beliefs AND the business.

Will you give me actual business strategy?

Yes.

When strategy is what the situation needs.

I’m not anti-strategy.

I’m anti pretending every woman needs the exact same strategy.

Sometimes we’re going to talk about your offer. Sometimes your messaging. Sometimes pricing. Sometimes visibility. Sometimes your numbers.

And sometimes I’m going to tell you to leave the fucking strategy alone because the strategy isn’t the problem.

What can I bring to coaching?

The business.

Bring the offer that isn’t selling. The price you’re thinking about changing. The client situation. The content. The idea. The launch. The opportunity. The money decision.

The thing you’re avoiding. The thing you keep changing. The decision you can’t tell whether to trust. The belief you caught yourself repeating.

If it affects the way you’re running your business or building the life you want, bring it.

What if I don’t know what my “block” is?

Perfect.

You don’t need to diagnose yourself before you get coached.

Tell me what’s happening.

I’ll ask questions.

We’ll figure out what actually needs attention.

And sometimes there isn’t a fucking block. Sometimes we need to fix something in the business.

Yes.

This is a growing vault.

As I create new work and see recurring needs inside the room, the library will continue expanding.

What is the Business Vault?

A growing collection of practical trainings, tools and resources around actually running and growing your business.

Do I need to be “good” at manifestation?

No.

But you should be open to looking at what you believe is available to you and how those beliefs affect the decisions you make.

And you need to be willing to act.

Because there will be no sitting around waiting for the universe to do your marketing.

What is your coaching style?

Direct.

I’m going to tell you what I see.

Sometimes that means challenging a belief.

Sometimes it means asking why the hell you’re about to change something that was working.

Sometimes it means telling you the strategy needs work.

I’m not interested in making you dependent on me or making every decision for you.

I want you to get better at seeing your own business clearly and making strong decisions without constantly needing somebody else to tell you what to do.

Will this guarantee that I make more money?

No.

Nobody can responsibly guarantee what your business will make.

I can coach you. I can help you see things you may not be seeing from inside the business. I can challenge the assumptions you’re treating like facts. I can give you strategic input. I can help you work through decisions. I can give you tools for the subconscious side of the work. I can teach you what I know.

You still have to run the fucking business.

Your results depend on your decisions, execution, market, experience, consistency and plenty of factors neither you nor I completely control.
