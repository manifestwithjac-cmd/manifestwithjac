The Method

The Quantum
Manifestation Formula™

Five steps. Same process every time. Works for anything — love, a new body, a baby, a new life. The process never changes.

1
Step One

Get clear on what you desire

Not what looks good on someone else's life. Not what you think you're supposed to want. What do YOU actually desire — and how does it feel to have it? Get so specific you can feel it in your body. What does your everyday life look like when it's already yours? Your subconscious needs a clear, felt target. Vague desires get vague results.

Practice feeling it — not just naming it
2
Step Two

Notice what comes up

When you really feel into having what you desire — what happens in your body? Does it feel like a hell yes, this is mine? Or does it feel a little scary, uncomfortable, like yeah right? That reaction is your self concept showing you exactly what it believes you're available for. That's the block. And now that you can see it — you can clear it.

You can never outmanifest your self concept
But what if I can't identify my block?

Sometimes nothing obvious comes up — you just feel numb, blank, or stuck. That blankness IS data. It usually means the block lives deeper than your conscious mind can access, which is exactly why surface-level methods never move it. This is the work we do together. Inside House of Her I'll help you find it, name it, and clear it at the root.

3
Step Three

Assume it's already yours

This is the law of assumption. You stop chasing the desire and assume it's already done. Instead of "I want this so bad" — you shift and transmute that energy into "I am so grateful this is already mine." You live from the end result. The universe doesn't respond to what you say — it responds to what you actually believe is true. And what you believe is what gets mirrored back.

Transmute want into gratitude for already having it
What if it just feels fake?

It will at first — and that's completely normal. If you've spent years believing something isn't possible for you, your body isn't going to flip a switch overnight. That tight, forced feeling when you try to feel grateful for something you don't have yet? That's your nervous system saying it doesn't feel safe yet. That's exactly why step four exists. The nervous system work is what makes this step feel true — calm, settled, real. Not forced.

Step Four — The Most Missed Piece

Do the daily nervous system work

Your nervous system has been conditioned by every experience you've ever had. So even when you consciously say "this is already mine" — underneath, your subconscious might be running a completely different story. Your body tenses. The old pattern kicks in. Limiting beliefs come up. Your brain loves homeostasis. It loves to keep you in what feels safe and familiar — even when familiar means stuck.

The daily nervous system work — breathwork, EFT tapping, subconscious recoding, subliminals — is your energetic gym. You go to the physical gym to work on your physical body. This is the gym for your energy. What you normalize will materialize.

This is what makes assuming it's yours actually feel true

It looks different every single day — and that's the point. Some days it's 10 minutes of quiet meditation in the morning. Another day it's a full breathwork session. Some days it's a quick EFT round before bed. Some days it's just holding the frequency and choosing not to spiral. There's no one thing you must do every day. It's about leaning in when you need the support and showing up consistently — even when it's small.

5
Step Five

Take action from her —
not toward her

Aligned action only. You don't obsess. You don't force. You never take action from a place of panic or lack. You move when it feels exciting, happy, passionate — when it's a genuine yes in your body. Your energetic state of being is what calls in your desire, not the action itself. So you take action based on the feeling, not based on what you think you're supposed to be doing. Every decision comes from that new identity — and reality rearranges to match who you're being, not what you're doing.

Aligned action feels exciting. Never forced.

And finally — ignore your current circumstances.

Your current reality is just old data. It's a reflection of old beliefs and old decisions — not who you are anymore, and not where you're going. When things feel like they're falling apart after you start this work, that's actually a really good sign. Your life is clearing out everything that's no longer aligned with your highest timeline.
